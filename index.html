Karnataka is home to beautiful forests, mountains, rivers, and protected wildlife areas. Explore the state's wildlife sanctuaries and discover the amazing animals, birds, insects, reptiles, and plants that live in these natural habitats.

About Us

Discover Karnataka's Wildlife

Wildlife sanctuaries are protected areas created to conserve animals and their natural habitats. Karnataka has several important sanctuaries that protect forests and support a rich variety of wildlife.

Our website helps visitors explore these sanctuaries, learn about wildlife, and understand the importance of conservation.

Conservation

Protect Our Wildlife

Wildlife is threatened by habitat loss, deforestation, poaching, pollution, forest fires, and climate change.

We can protect wildlife by:

  • Protecting forests and habitats.
  • Avoiding plastic and litter.
  • Never feeding wild animals.
  • Following sanctuary rules.
  • Maintaining a safe distance from wildlife.
  • Avoiding loud noises.
  • Supporting responsible tourism.
  • Spreading awareness.

"Protect Wildlife. Preserve Nature. Secure the Future."
